ScribeWorks

A scribe’s precision toolset for quick vector studies, measured layouts and clear proposals. A compact 2D editor, not a full DWG CAD system.

Draw: click points. Polyline: Enter to finish, C to close. Arc: center, start, then end (counterclockwise). Dimension: two endpoints, then label position. Text: click an anchor. Esc cancels.

Precision: type 1000,500, @500,0 or @1000<45 and Enter. Coordinates use X right, Y up. Object snaps include endpoints, midpoints, circle centers/quadrants and line intersections. Grid snap is fallback; ortho constrains from the last point.

Edit: click to select; Shift-click adds/removes. Move/copy: choose tool, then base and destination. Rotate/scale/mirror use selection bounds center. Offset supports lines, rectangles and circles; positive line offset is left of its direction. Trim removes the clicked line segment between intersections with other lines. Delete removes selected objects. Locked layers cannot be edited.

Navigate: wheel to zoom at cursor; middle-drag or Pan tool to pan. Ctrl Z undo, Ctrl Shift Z redo, Ctrl S save. L line, P polyline, R rectangle, O circle, A arc, D dimension, T text, V select, F fit.

Structure + MEP: Open the technical desk for members, columns, symbols, hatch, preliminary sizing references and schedules. Hatch selected closed boundaries; symbols and members are grouped. Save retains all component metadata. Use the technical desk’s Ungroup button for component editing.

Files: Save/Open use editable ScribeWorks JSON. SVG and DXF are exports only; DXF dimensions are exploded into lines/text. Print / PDF opens the browser print dialog; choose A3 landscape, 100% size for fixed scales. Units relabel coordinates; they do not convert geometry. Save a .scribe file for a durable backup.

ScribeWorks / Technical desk

Structural studies · MEP coordination · drawing references

Structural & service runs

Creates editable grouped vector geometry on a discipline layer. Repeat connected segments; Esc finishes. Segments meet at their centerline endpoints; fittings and junction joins are not automatic.

Columns & footings

Plan geometry only. These presets are drafting examples, not approved sizes or verified steel sections. Material grade, reinforcement, loads and connections require project engineering.

Reusable symbol library

Placement settings

Pick a symbol, then click in the drawing. Symbols remain active for repeated placement. Select, move, copy or rotate the group; ungroup for individual component edits.

Schematic project symbols, not a certified ISO/IEC symbol library. Confirm the legend and conventions with your project team.

Hatch selected boundaries

Vector-first patterns

Select rectangles, circles or simple closed polylines before opening this panel. Patterns are clipped to each selected boundary and exported as real linework in SVG, DXF and PDF.

Hatches are grouped with their boundary and move together. Ungroup to edit individual lines. After changing a boundary, remove and reapply its hatch. Each boundary is filled independently; nested hole subtraction and self-intersecting boundaries are not supported.

Hatch generation is limited to 4,000 strokes per boundary to keep the editor responsive. Increase spacing for large regions.

Preliminary calculations in SI units. Results are not code checks or final equipment/member selections. Inputs are illustrative, not recommended design limits.

MEP · flow and electrical load

Enter values and calculate.

Flow uses A = Q/v. Circular bore D = √(4A/π). Duct sizing excludes friction, fittings, noise and fan pressure. Pipe sizing excludes pressure, friction, material wall thickness, drainage and fixture-unit loading. Electrical current excludes diversity, motor starting, conductor ampacity and protective-device selection.

Structure · elastic beam study

Enter section properties and calculate.

M = wL²/8 · V = wL/2 · δ = 5wL⁴/(384EI) · σ = M/Z. Includes only the entered load; include self-weight yourself. Assumes a linear elastic prismatic beam. No buckling, shear capacity, vibration, connection, fire or load-combination verification. Deflection target is user-defined.
